# Weathered cedar — v1

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool, not CLI/API fallback. Saved as weathered-cedar-v1.png and embedded into assets.js for offline use. This is an AI-generated color texture, not a measured PBR scan. Beam/plank geometry and UVs remain procedural.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: tileable timber BASE COLOR material for the beams, wall planks and wooden terrace of a cozy coastal cabin, not a finished scene. Generate a high-resolution square orthographic photograph-like material swatch, edge-to-edge continuous seasoned cedar wood. Straight long wood fibers run VERTICALLY from bottom to top. Warm desaturated medium taupe-brown with subtle gray salt-weathering, delicate irregular annual growth lines, small shallow pores, a few hairline checks following the grain, only one or two very understated small elongated knots. Realistic tactile aged timber, cared for and smooth to the touch rather than damaged or splintered. Fine detail with gentle broad tonal variation. Even flat neutral diffuse illumination, base color only, NO directional shadow, NO shine, NO orange/golden lighting baked into the texture. NO individual planks, NO seams or board divisions, NO end-grain rings, NO isolated wooden object, no background, no text, no logos, no watermark. Seamless matching edges in both axes, soft restrained contrast with no dominant central motif, suitable for repeated UV mapping on slender timber members. Not painted, not a stylized CGI wood texture. Ideally 2048x2048.
